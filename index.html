Mediation, step by step

A simulated study you can walk through. Press Next, or Play to let it run. Change the scenario to see how each result looks.

All data are simulated from the scenario you pick, with unstandardized effects and noise of 1. The bootstrap here is the percentile method with 5,000 resamples. A significant indirect effect is statistical evidence; calling it causal also requires the right design (X before M before Y, no unmeasured confounding of M and Y).
